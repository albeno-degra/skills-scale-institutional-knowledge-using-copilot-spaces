# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises. Depending on project size, a role may be filled by a dedicated person or assigned to an existing team member; the project team should name an accountable owner for each responsibility.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

## Additional Cross-Functional Roles

### UX Designer / User Researcher

#### Role Summary
The UX Designer / User Researcher helps the team understand user needs and turn them into accessible, usable experiences.

#### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research, then share findings and their implications
- Create or maintain experience flows, prototypes, and usability and accessibility criteria
- Validate designs with users and capture issues for the backlog

#### Goals
- Ensure delivery decisions are grounded in user needs
- Identify usability concerns early enough to address them

#### Collaboration and Accountability
- Partners with the Product Manager on discovery, problem definition, and prioritization; the Product Manager remains accountable for product priorities
- Works with Developers and the Technical Lead on feasibility and with QA/Testing on usability validation
- Coordinates research timing and shares findings with the Project Manager for planning and stakeholder updates

#### Typical Communication
- Research readouts, design reviews, and user-flow documentation
- Usability findings and acceptance criteria shared with product, development, and QA

### Technical Lead / Architect

#### Role Summary
The Technical Lead / Architect guides technical design and integration so the solution is coherent, maintainable, and testable.

#### Responsibilities
- Lead technical design and document significant architecture and integration decisions
- Identify technical dependencies, risks, and constraints; contribute to estimates
- Establish or clarify technical standards and support design and code reviews
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on testability and technical validation needs

#### Goals
- Enable reliable implementation and integration
- Make technical trade-offs and risks visible early

#### Collaboration and Accountability
- Coordinates with the Project Manager on dependencies, estimates, and technical risks
- Advises the Product Manager on feasibility and trade-offs; the Product Manager owns product scope and priority
- Guides Developers on implementation and collaborates with QA/Testing on test strategy without replacing their validation responsibilities

#### Typical Communication
- Technical design documents, integration plans, and code reviews
- Early escalation of technical risks and decisions affecting scope or timing

### Security and Privacy Lead

#### Role Summary
The Security and Privacy Lead helps the team identify and address security and privacy requirements and risks throughout delivery.

#### Responsibilities
- Identify applicable security and privacy requirements and assess project risks
- Advise on appropriate safeguards and coordinate security reviews and follow-up
- Track findings to resolution or explicitly communicate residual risks and escalation needs

#### Goals
- Integrate security and privacy considerations into planning, implementation, and release decisions
- Make ownership and status of security and privacy risks clear

#### Collaboration and Accountability
- Works with Developers and QA/Testing to plan reviews, validate safeguards, and track remediation; implementation teams remain responsible for their changes
- Informs the Project Manager of risks, mitigations, and dependencies for the risk register and escalation
- Advises the Product Manager on impact to scope and release readiness; product and release decisions remain with their designated owners

#### Typical Communication
- Security and privacy requirements, review findings, and remediation status
- Timely escalation of material risks to the Project Manager and Product Manager

### Release / Operations Lead

#### Role Summary
The Release / Operations Lead coordinates operational readiness and the transition from delivery to supported production use.

#### Responsibilities
- Coordinate deployment readiness, operational handoff, monitoring, and rollback planning
- Confirm runbooks, support ownership, and post-deployment verification needs are documented
- Coordinate incident handoff and capture operational follow-up actions

#### Goals
- Make releases supportable and observable
- Reduce deployment risk and improve response to production issues

#### Collaboration and Accountability
- Partners with the Project Manager on release timing, dependencies, and readiness tracking
- Coordinates with Developers and QA/Testing on deployment steps, smoke tests, monitoring, and rollback validation
- Aligns with the Product Manager on release impact and with Stakeholders on operational handoff and launch communications

#### Typical Communication
- Release-readiness checklists, deployment plans, and operational runbooks
- Post-deployment verification results and incident or follow-up updates
