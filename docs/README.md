# OctoAcme Project Management Processes

OctoAcme starts initiatives by validating the business need, agreeing on measurable outcomes, identifying stakeholders, and documenting an initial timeline, risks, and resource needs. Once the work is approved, planning turns it into a prioritized, estimated backlog with acceptance criteria, a Definition of Done, milestones, dependencies, and a QA approach.

During execution, teams deliver small increments and track work on a project board. The Project Manager coordinates delivery, risks, and communication; the Product Manager owns outcomes and priorities; developers implement and test; QA validates acceptance criteria; and stakeholders provide input and approvals. Regular standups, weekly delivery or PM–Product syncs, stakeholder updates, and milestone demos keep progress and blockers visible, with risks escalated through the agreed paths.

Quality is supported by unit and integration tests, end-to-end smoke tests for critical flows, automated CI and security scans, and pull-request review. Releases require acceptance criteria and CI checks to pass, a rollback or mitigation plan, staging smoke tests, and post-deployment verification. Retrospectives after sprints, releases, or incidents turn lessons into owned, measurable follow-up actions.
